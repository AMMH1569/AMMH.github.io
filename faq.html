Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our customers ask most.

Home / FAQ

How do I order products?

Browse the shop, add the items you need to your cart and complete the checkout form. You can also message us on the phone number listed in the contact section and our team will place the order for you.

Do you provide delivery?

Yes. We deliver within Yangon on the same or next working day, and nationwide within 2-4 working days. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard delivery.

Are products suitable for everyone?

Our products are general wellness products, not medicines, and they are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, taking medication or managing a health condition, please speak with your doctor or our pharmacist first.

What payment methods are available?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, and major mobile wallets. Card payments are available in store.

Can I get advice before buying?

Yes. Call or message us and our pharmacy team will help you choose a suitable product. For medical concerns we will always recommend speaking with your doctor.

How do I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery in their original packaging. Contact us first so we can arrange collection.
