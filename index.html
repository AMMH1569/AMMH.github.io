Welcome to Samaia

A little piece of Georgia in London.

Samaia is an intimate and relaxed Georgian restaurant on Castelnau, Barnes. Our kitchen celebrates the rich flavours, generous portions and timeless traditions of Georgian cooking.

From freshly prepared khinkali and golden khachapuri to beautifully grilled meats and comforting homemade dishes, every plate is made to bring people together around the table.

Made with heart. Served with warmth.
